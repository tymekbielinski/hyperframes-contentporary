# C1 — Flow strip

Rule: `standards/formats/long-form.md` (catalogue row C1). This card holds the evidence and the quality bar; the rule stays in the format profile.

## Quality bar

- Nodes are UI-like objects, not boxes: dark glass tiles styled as the real product (YouTube Studio title field, Create / Publish buttons, metric chips with the real labels), or a real logo.
- One node per spoken phrase; each connector draws (thin 1–2 px line or hand-drawn curve) before the next node arrives.
- Verdict marks sit on the links, not in a headline: a small red ✕ on the wrong link, a green ✓ on the right one (s039).
- The flow lands on a payoff that is real UI (a video card with 'No views' in a red block) plus a short line with a signature-script accent word in red.
- A large, blurred, dim brand mark (the YouTube logo) behind the nodes gives depth without competing.
- Camera moves only to follow a new node (legs ≈ 0.8–1 s) and holds ≥ 0.8 s on the full diagram.
